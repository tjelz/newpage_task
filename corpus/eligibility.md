# Who can enroll

A patient can enroll only when every point below is true.

- They are 18 or older.
- A prescriber has confirmed moderate or severe Northstar syndrome.
- They are not pregnant and are not planning a pregnancy.
- The prescriber has sent a signed enrollment form.

The patient signs the form. A carer cannot enroll on the patient's behalf. Patients under 18 are not eligible, including when a parent agrees.
