# Nurse line hours

The nurse line is open on weekdays from 08:00 to 18:00 UK time, and on Saturday from 09:00 to 13:00 UK time. The line is closed on Sunday and on UK bank holidays.

Outside those hours, leave a voicemail. It is returned on the next working morning. The number is 0800 111 440.

# Delivery desk

The delivery desk uses the same hours as the nurse line. A travel letter needs five working days' notice.
