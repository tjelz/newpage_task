# Program overview

Northstar Patient Support is a fictional program in this demo. It is not a real medicine and not a real clinic.

Northstar arranges home delivery of Northstar capsules for enrolled patients, answers program questions on the nurse line, and writes travel letters when an enrolled patient is away from home for more than three nights.

Northstar is not an emergency service. Trouble breathing, swelling of the face or throat, or a widespread rash is covered in the adverse-event guide. That guide says to call emergency services first.
