# How to take Northstar capsules

The usual dose is 40 mg, one capsule, once each morning with food.

# Missed dose

If the morning dose is missed and it is still within 6 hours of the usual time, take that capsule with food. If more than 6 hours have passed, skip the missed dose. Do not take two capsules the next morning.

# Storage

Keep the bottle closed, below 25°C, and out of sunlight. Do not keep the capsules in a bathroom.
